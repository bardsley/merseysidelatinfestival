      Timba Británica is a dynamic UK-based band inspired by the energy, complexity and irresistible groove of Cuban timba. Bringing together powerful brass, driving percussion, rich vocal harmonies and an infectious rhythm section, the band creates a bold live sound designed to move both the body and the spirit.

      At the heart of Timba Británica is a deep love of Cuban music and its ability to bring people together. Timba combines elements of son, salsa, jazz, funk and Afro-Cuban rhythms, creating music that is exciting, adventurous and full of life. The band celebrates this tradition while adding its own distinctly British character, shaped by the diverse musical backgrounds and influences of its members.

      Their performances are energetic, spontaneous and made for dancing. Intricate arrangements meet fiery solos, call-and-response vocals and constantly shifting rhythms, giving every show a sense of excitement and connection. Whether playing to experienced salsa dancers or introducing new audiences to the sound of timba, the band creates a welcoming atmosphere where everyone can join in.

      Timba Británica is more than a tribute to a musical style. It is a celebration of collaboration, cultural exchange and the shared joy of live music. With passion, precision and plenty of groove, the band brings the spirit of Havana to British stages—one unforgettable performance at a time.
